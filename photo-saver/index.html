Photo Saver

Pick photos from Google Photos and save them to your Downloads folder, each one kept under the size limit.

One-time setup

Paste the client ID from your Google Cloud project. Its Authorized JavaScript origin must be https://danbarber81-hub.github.io. The ID is saved in this browser only.

Save photos

Photos already under the limit are saved untouched. Larger ones (and HEIC photos) are saved as JPG, shrunk only as much as needed. Videos are skipped.
