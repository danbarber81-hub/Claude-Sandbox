# Claude Sandbox

## Photo Saver

A small web app that lets you pick photos from Google Photos and save them to your Downloads folder, each kept under a size limit (3.8 MB by default).

Live at: https://danbarber81-hub.github.io/Claude-Sandbox/photo-saver/

Everything runs in your browser. Your Google client ID and sign-in stay in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere except Google.
